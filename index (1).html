Cuts and styling

Shape, length and finish.

Women’s cut

What it is. A cut shaped to your hair type, face and the length you want.

Good for. Trims, a new shape, or growing out a cut.

Good to know. Bring a photo and say how much time you want to spend styling. Ask if a wash and finish is included.

Men’s cut

What it is. A scissor or clipper cut, from classic styles to fades, with neckline and edges tidied.

Good for. Regular upkeep or a new look.

Good to know. Show a photo, or tell your stylist the clipper lengths you like.

Blow-dry

What it is. Your hair is styled with a dryer for a smooth, voluminous or wavy finish.

Good for. Events, or a fresh finish without a cut.

Good to know. The style usually lasts until your next wash, sometimes a few days.

Consultation

What it is. A conversation about your hair and your goals, before any work starts.

Good for. Big changes, or when you don’t know what to book.

Good to know. It avoids surprises. Ask your stylist if the consultation is free or paid.

Color

From soft and natural to bold.

Balayage

What it is. Color painted by hand for soft, sun-kissed lightness that blends into your roots.

Good for. A natural look with low upkeep.

Good to know. It grows out gently, so you can wait longer between visits. Ask for a time estimate, as it can take several hours.

Highlights

What it is. Selected strands are lightened for a brighter, more defined look.

Good for. Adding brightness and contrast.

Good to know. Regrowth shows sooner than with balayage, so touch-ups may come earlier.

Blonde transformation

What it is. Lightening your hair to a blonde shade.

Good for. A big change to blonde.

Good to know. Dark or previously colored hair often needs more than one session. Ask for an honest plan and aftercare advice.

Creative color

What it is. Vivid, pastel or fashion shades such as pink, blue or copper.

Good for. Standing out and showing your style.

Good to know. Bright shades fade faster. Ask which shampoo and washing habits keep them fresh.

Specialist treatments

For particular hair types and long-lasting results.

Curly hair

What it is. Cutting and styling designed for curls and coils, so they hold their shape.

Good for. Curls that feel frizzy or shapeless.

Good to know. Ask how your stylist likes you to arrive, since many curl specialists prefer a particular starting state.

Extensions

What it is. Added hair for extra length or fullness.

Good for. Length, volume or a temporary change.

Good to know. Methods differ. Ask which one is used, how long it lasts and how to care for it.

Braids and locs

What it is. Protective and decorative styles, including installing and maintaining them.

Good for. Low-maintenance styles you can wear for a while.

Good to know. Installs can take a long time. Ask about the hair length needed and how to look after the style.

Keratin

What it is. A smoothing treatment that reduces frizz and makes hair easier to manage.

Good for. Frizzy or hard-to-style hair.

Good to know. Results fade over weeks to months. Your stylist will explain the aftercare rules.

Perms

What it is. A chemical treatment that sets waves or curls.

Good for. Lasting wave or extra volume.

Good to know. Tell your stylist about any past color or chemical treatments.

Straightening

What it is. A long-lasting treatment that makes hair straight and smooth.

Good for. Curly or wavy hair you want straight for a long time.

Good to know. The result lasts until new hair grows in. Share your chemical history first.

Quick answers

Do I pay on this website?

No. This site only introduces the stylists. You book and pay on your stylist’s own booking page.

Who sets the prices and cancellation rules?

Each stylist is independent and sets their own prices, schedule and rules.

Can I book with more than one stylist?

Yes. Each booking is separate, so you can see different stylists for different services.

What if I’m not sure what to book?

Start with a consultation, or choose the closest service and add a note when you book.
